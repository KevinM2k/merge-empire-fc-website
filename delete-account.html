Delete your account

Merge Empire FC · Kevin Matthews

What "your account" means here

Merge Empire FC has no account of its own. You sign in with an account you already have — your Google account or your Apple ID — so that your progress can be backed up to the cloud and your results can appear on the global leaderboards. Signing in creates a record on our side, held against that sign-in, and this page is how you ask us to delete it.

Deleting that record does not touch your Google or Apple account, which remains entirely yours. You can start playing again at any time by signing in afresh.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below, using the email address of the Google or Apple account you sign in with — that address is how we find your record.
  2. Write Delete my account in the message, along with anything else you want us to know.
  3. We will delete your data and confirm by reply, within 30 days of the request.
✓ Request sent. We’ll confirm by email once your data has been deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record — the account identifier we hold, and the email address Google or Apple gave us when you signed in.
  • Your cloud save — your club, your squad, your season progress and your settings, as backed up to our servers.
  • Your leaderboard entries — every one of them, across all ranking periods and regions.
  • Your messages — any feedback or support requests you have sent us.

Deleting only some of your data

You do not have to delete everything. Use the same form to ask us to remove just one part of what we hold — your leaderboard entries, or your cloud save — and to leave the rest as it is. Say which you mean in the message, and your account stays open either way.

If you only want to disappear from the rankings without deleting anything, you can do that yourself: turn off leaderboard visibility in the game's settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Anonymous usage and crash data. This is recorded against a device identifier rather than against your account, and cannot be traced back to you once your account is gone. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, usage analytics for no longer than 14 months.
  • Purchase records. In-app purchases are processed by the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store, and the record of them belongs to those stores rather than to us. Both retain it to meet their own legal and tax obligations. We never receive or store your payment details.
  • Backups. Deleted data is cleared from our backups within 30 days of the deletion.

The save on your device

The game also keeps a copy of your progress on the phone itself, so that it can be played offline. Deleting your account does not remove that local copy, and nothing from it is sent to us afterwards. To remove it as well, uninstall the game or clear its data through your device settings.
