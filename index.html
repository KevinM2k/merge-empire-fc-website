What's underneath

It looks like a merge game.
It plays like a manager.

Most football merge games stop at the merging. This one keeps going.

01

Merge to dominate

Put two identical cards side by side and they fuse into a stronger version. Every merge is a step towards a squad of Legends.

02

Match days

League fixtures across seven divisions. Top the table and earn promotion, or let the squad stagnate and go down.

03

Idle club income

Your club earns around the clock, even while the app is shut. Come back to a treasury and spend it on packs, upgrades or scouting.

04

Build your club

Stadium, training ground, sponsors and merchandise. Every tier you build unlocks better talent and higher revenue.

05

Deadline day

Bids land, loans expire and agents push, on a window that actually closes.

06

Global leaderboards

Rank in your division, your country or the world. Signing in is optional — the game plays fine without an account.

The ladder

Seven divisions.
One destiny.

Club building

More than
just players.

Build and upgrade the club itself. Each asset tier raises revenue and unlocks higher-tier talent.

Stadium

From a modest ground to a world-class arena. Each tier lifts matchday revenue and signals your ambition to rivals.

Training ground

Better facilities speed up development, unlock higher-tier card slots and give your squad the edge in tight matches.

Sponsors

Offers arrive with terms worth weighing. The right deal funds a window; the wrong one ties you up for a season.

Merch store

Sell the club's brand worldwide. A thriving shop compounds passive income even while you are offline.
