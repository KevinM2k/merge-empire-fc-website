How to Play

Everything a Football Manager Needs

Four core pillars that will keep you coming back for just one more match

Merge to Dominate

Place two identical cards side by side to fuse them into a more powerful version. Every merge edges you closer to fielding an unstoppable team of Legends.

Idle Club Income

Your club earns coins 24/7 — even while you're away. Return to a treasury bursting with coins and spend them on new card packs, upgrades, or scouting.

Simulate Match Days

Compete in league fixtures across 7 divisions. Win matches, top the table, and earn promotion — or risk relegation if you let the squad stagnate.

Build Your Club

Construct and upgrade your stadium, training ground, media centre, and merch store. Each asset tier unlocks more powerful players and higher revenues.
